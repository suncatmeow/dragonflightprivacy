Battle Mage: Dragonflight Privacy Policy

Developer: Suncat
Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how the offline version of Battle Mage: Dragonflight handles information.

Information handled by the game

The game stores progress and preferences locally on your device so you can continue your adventure. It does not require an account. In the offline version described by this policy, Suncat does not collect or receive your gameplay records, personal information, or device identifiers through the game.

Sharing and advertising

The offline game does not transmit your saved progress to Suncat or third-party advertisers and does not include advertising or analytics services. If a future version introduces online features or data collection, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local progress and settings remain in the app's storage until you remove them. You can clear the app's storage through Android settings. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage. If Android or your device provider backs up app data, backup retention and restoration depend on their settings and policies; clearing local storage may not delete a previously created backup.

Optional external links

If the app allows you to open an external website, that site has its own privacy practices. Its handling of information is separate from the offline game.
