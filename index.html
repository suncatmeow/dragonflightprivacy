Battle Mage: Dragonflight Privacy Policy

Developer: Suncat
Effective date: October 14, 2026
Applies to: Android release 1.0

Battle Mage: Dragonflight is an offline, single-player game. You do not need an account or sign-in to play. This policy explains what the game saves, how Android backups may affect that data, and how to contact Suncat.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your preferences, best score, campaign checkpoints, and recent in-game upgrade offers in the app's local storage. Preferences include difficulty, sound, graphics, controls, and the gentle-effects setting. Checkpoints contain game progress and character state. This information lets the game remember your settings and continue your adventure.

The game does not ask you to provide personal information. Suncat does not receive your save files, gameplay history, or personal or device identifiers through gameplay.

Permissions and network communication

Android release 1.0 does not request Internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or shared-storage permissions. Its game content, graphics engine, and audio are included in the app and loaded locally. The game does not upload progress to a Suncat server.

The Android app blocks navigation to external websites. The game content contains optional music-site and Instagram links, but those links do not establish an online service used by this Android release. If you visit those websites separately, their privacy policies apply.

Advertising, analytics, and platform services

The game does not integrate advertising, analytics, or developer-operated remote crash reporting. It does not sell gameplay data or send it to advertisers.

The app uses Android's WebView and support libraries to run the local game. Google Play handles app distribution and updates separately, under Google's Privacy Policy. Android backup services are described below.

Android backups and device transfers

This release allows Android backup and does not exclude game data through custom backup rules. Depending on your Android version, device provider, backup settings, and system eligibility rules, app data may be backed up to your Google account or another device-provider backup service, or transferred to a new device.

These backups and transfers are performed by the operating system or device provider, separately from the game's offline operation. Suncat does not operate the backup service or receive its backup copies. Backup security, retention, restoration, and deletion follow the relevant provider's settings and policies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Game saves remain in the app's local storage until they are replaced or cleared. To delete them, open Android Settings, select Apps, select Dragonflight, then choose Storage or Storage & cache and Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes saved progress and preferences. Uninstalling also removes the app's local storage.

Deleting local data or uninstalling does not necessarily remove an existing system backup. A backup may restore app data after reinstallation or a device transfer. Use your device's or backup provider's controls to manage those copies.

The game uses app-local storage rather than a developer-hosted save database. Its Android wrapper also disables direct WebView access to device files and content providers.

Support and privacy questions

Contact Suncat at suncat.data@gmail.com.

If you email Suncat, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are received through Gmail so your inquiry can be read and answered. This correspondence is separate from game data and remains in the support mailbox until deleted. You can request deletion by emailing the same address. Clearing the game or uninstalling it does not delete email correspondence. Gmail's handling of email is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
